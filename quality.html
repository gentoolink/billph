Quality Assurance and Quality Control

Ensuring the highest standards in every project.

Construction Quality Assurance Plan

Our company employs a practicing Statistician (University of Calgary, Post-Graduate Studies) to design a Quality Assurance Plan for each construction project.

Quality Control Sampling

Our company practices Quality Control sampling on critical construction items and practices (piping torque, soldering and/or welding piping joints, piping expansion couplings, Reinforced Concrete Construction (RCC) rebar welding and tie-ins, metal or wooden form design and implementation) according to guidelines documented in ISO 2859 Quality Control sampling standard.
