Construction Project Management

Certified project management that keeps plumbing and mechanical work on schedule and on budget.

Certified Construction Project Management

What we do

Bill's Plumbing and Heating is certified to provide construction project management, with credentialed staff who bring more than 20 years of field experience to every job.

We plan, schedule, cost and supervise the mechanical side of construction projects, and we can take on wider site management where a project calls for it. The result is fewer surprises, clear reporting and a schedule everyone can work to.

Project management services

Project scheduling

Delivered in the format your project team already works with.

  • Gantt charts
  • Critical Path Method (CPM)
  • Program Evaluation Review Technique (PERT)
  • Agile methods for construction

Cost, reporting and resources

Keeping the numbers, the materials and the people lined up.

  • Project cost estimation and tracking
  • Reporting on site material delivery, preparation and handling
  • Sourcing critical skilled trades
  • Progress presentations and displays

Site safety and environment

Managed from the first day on site.

  • Site safety management
  • Spill prevention and remediation

Quality built in

Every project we manage gets a written Quality Assurance Plan and ISO 2859 sampling on critical items.
