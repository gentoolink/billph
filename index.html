Our Work

A few recent plumbing and heating installs. Every job is built to code and inspected where required.

Geo-exchange Heating

Design, installation and maintenance of geo-exchange systems for homes and commercial buildings.

Geo-exchange systems use the stable temperature underground to heat and cool a building at a fraction of the running cost of conventional systems. They are efficient and long lasting, but they need regular, knowledgeable service to stay that way. We handle routine maintenance, diagnostics and repairs so your system keeps performing and your energy bills stay low.
